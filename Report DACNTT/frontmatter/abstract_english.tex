\begin{center}
{\fontsize{15pt}{18pt}\selectfont \textbf{BUILDING A HYBRID RECOMMENDATION MODEL COMBINING MATRIX FACTORIZATION AND DEEP NEURAL NETWORKS}\par}
\vspace{0.5cm}
{\fontsize{16pt}{19.2pt}\selectfont \textbf{ABSTRACT}\par}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.2cm}

Matrix factorization predicts a customer's interest in an item with the inner product of two latent vectors learned from customer and item identifiers. On fashion data, which are extremely sparse and seasonal, both this linear form and the reliance on identifiers limit recommendation quality. This project proposes NeuMF-F, a hybrid model that keeps the two-branch structure of Neural Matrix Factorization (NeuMF), with a generalized matrix factorization (GMF) branch and a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) branch, and represents every customer and item by an identifier embedding combined with item, customer, sales and time features.

The model is evaluated on the H\&M fashion transaction dataset under a protocol designed to limit bias. Data are split by time, features use only information available before the prediction time, configurations are selected on a separate customer sample, and conclusions are drawn once on an independent customer sample under a pre-registered plan with statistical testing. The baselines are tuned with the same budget and grouped into single models, traditional machine learning and recommendation-specific models.

NeuMF-F is significantly better than identifier-only NeuMF, its MLP-F branch and the baselines of the comparison family, but it is not significantly different from its GMF-F branch alone or from the late-fusion model LateFusion-F. The improvement therefore comes mainly from the features, with time features contributing most, rather than from how the two branches are fused. A validation on the Amazon Beauty dataset confirms the benefit of the features, but on that dataset LateFusion-F significantly outperforms NeuMF-F. A supplementary analysis shows that NeuMF-F also significantly outperforms a traditional hybrid of content-based and collaborative filtering. The project also builds a demo system that illustrates the recommendations for each customer.
