\begin{center}
{\fontsize{15pt}{18pt}\selectfont \textbf{BUILDING A HYBRID RECOMMENDATION MODEL COMBINING MATRIX FACTORIZATION AND DEEP NEURAL NETWORKS}\par}
\vspace{0.5cm}
{\fontsize{16pt}{19.2pt}\selectfont \textbf{ABSTRACT}\par}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.2cm}

Matrix Factorization (MF) models user--item interactions with an inner product \parencite{koren2009matrix}, a linear combination that is argued to miss non-linear relationships \parencite{he2017ncf}. This project builds and evaluates the hybrid Neural Matrix Factorization (NeuMF) model, which combines a linear Generalized Matrix Factorization (GMF) branch with a non-linear Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) branch on implicit feedback, in two variants: trained from scratch and initialised from pre-trained GMF and MLP.

Experiments use the H\&M Personalized Fashion Recommendations dataset \parencite{hm2022kaggle}. From 31.8 million transactions, a reproducible customer-level sample is drawn (hm500k: 500,269 transactions of 21,599 customers with their full two-year history), user--item pairs are aggregated, a 10-core filter is applied (7,519 users, 10,345 items, 0.28\% density), and the data is split at a single global time point so that no model sees the future. All models -- NeuMF, its GMF and MLP branches, and the Random, Most Popular and BPR-MF baselines -- are tuned with the same budget on the validation set only; the test set is locked and every evaluation on it goes through a logged script, following a pre-registered plan. Before testing, each model is retrained on the union of the training and validation sets; this step was added after the first test run and is reported as a deviation from the plan, and the conclusions are the same in both runs. Evaluation uses full ranking over the whole catalogue, three seeds, and per-user Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with bootstrap confidence intervals and Holm correction.

On the test set, tuned BPR-MF has the highest mean NDCG@10; pre-trained NeuMF is on par with Most Popular, and the hybrid model selected on validation (NeuMF from scratch) scores lower still. None of the pre-registered comparisons reaches significance: NeuMF does not outperform a tuned MF baseline or its own GMF and MLP branches, and pre-training brings no measurable gain. In the early/late fusion taxonomy, NeuMF fuses the MF and DNN branches at the representation level (early fusion); a pure MF + DNN late fusion was not run, and a score-level blend of MF with NeuMF looked promising on validation but was not evaluated on the test set. This negative result is consistent with recent re-evaluations of NCF \parencite{rendle2020ncfmf, dacrema2019progress}: on sparse fashion transaction data, the added complexity of NeuMF is not repaid in accuracy. An inference-only demo (FastAPI) compares the models for individual customers, with recommendation latency measured on CPU.
